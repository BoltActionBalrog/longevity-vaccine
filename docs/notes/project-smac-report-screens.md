## Bake lessons (2026-09-04)
- The DL walker `t_dl_immfix` is reusable with `esi = table`; its VirtualProtect covers 0x429000..0x49C000 only — report-only rows need their own VP: t_rw_init_leave opens 0x49C000 + `vp_len()` (sized from the tables, 0x11000 since bake v1a; the 0xD000 constant crashed v1 once the kinds rows passed 0x4A9000).
- Unicorn parity harness: fresh Uc instances AV-kill the interpreter after ~90 on the development machine → cache one per g and `ctl_flush_tb()` before every run (translated-block cache serves stale stubs otherwise).
- The thick renderers cover a rect INCLUSIVELY; vanilla box_sprite is half-open → wrap with a helper that decrements R/B in place and restores (never touch the shrink-back constants, they share a register with the grow).
- `hlt`-terminated stubs leave eip = stub+1.
- A "half-open" dec helper around hollow_flat is NOT a law: hollow_flat's right/bottom stroke has a g-dependent floor phase (skill scaling-frames §5) — the dec cancelled it at 1600/1920 and over-corrected at 4K. The lawful fix is the t_dl_bmxt exact-fill pattern (g_faction_scratch_rect + g_fc_exact + R−1/B−1 copy); and every such helper needs its own g≤16 native branch (the thick renderers' no-op does not undo the helper's rect edit).
- Console chrome insets are register-loaded imm32 `mov reg, ±N` pairs with a RESTORE: scale the restore as one s(N) and derive the intermediate steps from it (s(a)+s(b) ≠ s(a+b) at g=22 → 1px creep per repaint).
