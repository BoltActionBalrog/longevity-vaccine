"We will not be hosting Thinker ourselves" (user, 2026-11-13) is about the author's download traffic and credit, not about Thinker bytes ever appearing in our files. User, 2026-11-14: "Shipping the .ini files in our installer is acceptable. The point was that we didn't want to be taking traffic and download credit away from the Thinker authors' repository and download links."

**Why:** respect for Thinker's author: players should download his builds from his GitHub releases (Dropbox only for a build he links himself).

**How to apply:** Thinker binaries/releases are always fetched from the author's own links, never mirrored by us (the maintainer may mirror one on their own GitHub only as a last resort). Small comparison data derived from Thinker (stock settings, hashes, file lists) may be compiled into our installer without asking. Related: [[feedback-thinker-conflicts-user-decides]].
